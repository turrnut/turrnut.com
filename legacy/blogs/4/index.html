By Turrnut on _________, 2026

How exactly should we think about death? How, if at all, should we prepare for the day when it will finally befall us?

This is what I will try to explore in this article. I may not fully give you the answer, but I do hope that I can become a part of this discussion and hopefully answer part of that question.
